Kyvo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026 · App package: com.itofi.kyvo

This policy explains how Kyvo (“the app”, “we”, “us”), published by itofi, handles information. Kyvo lets you run separate copies (“clones”) of apps that are already installed on your device, each in its own isolated space on your phone.

In short
  • Kyvo has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • Everything Kyvo stores stays on your device and is deleted when you remove a clone, clear the app’s data or uninstall Kyvo.
  • Apps you clone keep their own privacy policies and may send data to their own servers, just as they would outside Kyvo.

1. Information Kyvo stores on your device

To work, Kyvo keeps the following locally on your device only. None of it is sent to us.

WhatWhy
The list of apps installed on your deviceTo show which apps you can clone. Read when you open the app picker; not stored or transmitted.
Your clones and their names, icons and settingsSo your clones appear on the home screen the way you set them up.
Each clone’s app data (logins, files, caches)Each clone keeps its own data, separate from the original app and from other clones.
A randomly generated virtual device identity per clone (for example a virtual Android ID, IMEI, serial number and MAC address)So each clone looks like a separate device to the app inside it. These values are random, are not your device’s real identifiers and are erased with the clone.

2. Information we collect

None. Kyvo does not contain analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs, and it does not send any information about you or your device to us or to any third party.

3. Permissions

Kyvo requests a broad set of Android permissions (for example camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, phone and accounts). Kyvo itself does not use these to collect anything. They exist so that the apps you clone can use the same features they would use normally, such as a cloned messaging app taking a photo or a cloned map app reading your location. Android still asks you before granting sensitive permissions, and you can revoke them at any time in your device settings. Highlights:

  • Query all packages: to list the installed apps you can choose to clone.
  • Internet and network state: so cloned apps can go online. Kyvo does not use the network to send data to us.
  • Display over other apps, foreground service: to keep running clones working while they are in use.
  • Storage: so cloned apps can read and save files you give them.

4. Apps you clone

A cloned app is the original app running inside Kyvo. What it collects, and where it sends that information, is controlled by that app’s developer and governed by its own privacy policy, not this one. Kyvo does not read, intercept or store the content of cloned apps beyond keeping their data on your device so they keep working.

5. Data sharing and sale

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any information, because we do not receive any.

6. Data retention and deletion

All data lives on your device. You can delete it at any time:

  • Remove a clone in Kyvo to erase that clone and all its data.
  • Clear Kyvo’s data in Android Settings › Apps › Kyvo › Storage to erase everything.
  • Uninstall Kyvo to erase everything.

7. Security

Clone data is kept in Kyvo’s private app storage, which other apps on your device cannot access. Because nothing is transmitted to us, there is no copy of your data on our side to be exposed.

8. Children

Kyvo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
